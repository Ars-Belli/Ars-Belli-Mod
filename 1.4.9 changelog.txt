Patch 1.4.9

Checksum: 3ed0
Date: November 18th, 2026
Previous Patch: 1.4.8

Gameplay​

Government​
Fixed Parliament becoming impossible to call when none of the Estates' stored issues could be debated; issues now refresh monthly in that case.
Aligned Legislature no longer clears junior partner policies in laws the senior partner cannot access.
Fixed rulers being unable to marry Christian and other non-state-religion characters of the Ḏimmī estate in their court, such as at the Ottoman court.

Economy​
River and road bonuses to trade path cost no longer stack, harbors now reduce the trade cost of loading and unloading goods more, and the Maritime Presence description no longer claims it affects trade.
Customs Houses now make it more expensive, not cheaper, for foreign countries to buy goods from your markets.

Society​
Religious order holding bonuses, such as the Wardens' fort defense, are no longer lost while the holding is besieged or blockaded.
Artists can no longer create holy relic works of art.
Post-conquest refugees no longer flee to a capital the previous owner has lost, and Neutral cultures no longer count as discriminated.
A betrothed character no longer always moves to their partner's court when the younger of the two comes of age; as with a royal marriage, the proposing country's character stays.

Diplomacy​
Overlords can now use Intervene in War to help their Tributaries, such as one attacked during its own independence war.
Blocking a country from building in your territory no longer ends silently when your opinion of them rises above 100.
Fixed Castile keeping the permanent "Stand by our Portuguese allies" conquest malus against Portuguese areas after its alliance with Portugal ended.

Military​
Ships sunk in a naval battle can no longer be captured, so every captured ship in the battle result now joins the winning fleet.
Armies no longer become exiled on military-access territory after loading a save, hotjoining or rehosting.
Muster the Ghuzat now takes a quarter of the soldiers and 15% of the laborers from the chosen provinces over six months instead of nearly all of them, and can no longer take pops from your own subjects.
A patrolling fleet now holds its sea zone until maritime presence reaches 100% instead of leaving at 90%, and the Patrol the Seas description explains that each fleet holds one sea zone at a time.
A fleet in port now repairs at the better of the port's repair speed and its at-sea repair speed, so advances like Makeshift Repairs no longer make repairing at sea faster than in port.
A ship down to a sliver of hull that then founders is no longer counted as lost twice in the battle result.

Geopolitics​
Commission Conquistador no longer offers areas out of naval range of a port in your capital area or region, which cancelled the conquistador immediately.

Content​

Setup​
Fixed Kilwa starting the game with 4 "Potters' Guild" buildings in Rural Settlements, where they cannot be built.
Fixed Poland and the Commonwealth not becoming a Kingdom when formed from a lower-ranked country.
Added the missing parents of the Jalayirid, Shirvani, Muzaffarid and Hazaraspid rulers, who started the game with a parent absent.

Disasters​
Fixed the "Revolution!" disaster never starting, as the "Aspiration for Liberty" disaster could last indefinitely and blocked it. "Aspiration for Liberty" now ends after 20 years.

Events​
Fixed "The Iberian Wedding" event being able to start for rebel countries and to offer a rebel country as a wedding partner.
Fixed the "Attempted Assassination of Moronao by Tadayoshi" event not removing Ashikaga Tadayoshi from the cabinet when choosing to expel him.
Fixed the "The Northern Yuán" event, the "The Rise of a New Dynasty" event and the Manchu event trying to form Yuán or China while another country already held that tag, which left the formation half done.
Fixed the "Expansion of the Daimyo" event never giving the Shogun the Reassert Shogun Authority casus belli when a clan breaks away, leaving "Clan gains independence" without a casus belli to show.
Fixed the "Fraternal Struggle" regency never ending when the only remaining son of the previous ruler is underage, because the succession event had no valid option.
Fixed the "Advancement of the Clergy", "Clergy Losing Influence" and "Deteriorating Relations with the Clergy" events being able to fire for countries that have no Clergy estate.
Fixed several Nobles estate events being able to fire for countries that have no Nobles estate.
Fixed several Brahmins estate events being able to fire for countries that lack the Clergy or Nobles estates they affect.
Fixed two character estate events being able to fire for countries that have no Nobles estate.
The Coronation of the Aragonese ruler decision can no longer be repeated after the event fires, and is now dismissed when you abolish the tradition of coronating your ruler in Zaragoza.
The Byzantine event "Restoring the Varangian Guard" now displays the migration of peasants from Scandinavia to Constantinople.
The Granadan event "An opportunity in the struggle" now displays the foreign ruler in its text.
The Umayyad Pretender event chain now displays that rebels are created at the end.
The Iberian culture event "Skilled Mudéjar Artist" no longer highlights the word Christian in its option text.
Fixed the "Conversion of the Andalusi People" event converting and expelling the Sunni pops of every culture instead of only the Andalusi.
Fixed the heir marriage proposal event targeting betrothed characters, and its first option failing to create the marriage.
Fixed "The Return of the King" event never offering its regency options: its ruler requirement failed while Scotland had a regent, and it could not fire until the regency had already ended.
Fixed the "Our Share of the World" events showing an error instead of the colony location name in their description.
Fixed the Union of Crowns succession-fulfilled events leaving the pact modifier behind, which made the partners switch from Salic Law to Cognatic Primogeniture a few years later.
Fixed the requirements of the "Dragoș Vodă, Founder of Moldavia" event in the Events tab appearing to apply to Hungary's subjects instead of the Golden Horde's.
Rebalanced the "Corruption Among the Clergy" event so that ignoring the scandal for a share of the profits now also costs 7 Stability.
Fixed the "Declining Power of the Nobility" event reducing Nobles satisfaction instead of their power; it now applies a 15-year "Decline of Nobles Influence" modifier that lowers Nobles estate power by 10%.
Fixed the Polish-Lithuanian marriage proposal event never firing once the Teutonic Order no longer exists.
The "A Fiery End" event for Navarre now fires even when there is no heir, and only describes the heir taking the throne when Charles II died as king.
Halved the estate enrichment of the "Abuses by Estates" modifier from its event and shortened its duration from 5 to 2 years, making it less punishing.
Fixed the "Decline of the Merchant Classes", "Clergy Losing Influence" and "Declining Power of the Brāhmaṇa" events reducing estate satisfaction instead of estate power; they now apply a 15-year modifier that lowers the estate's power by 10%.

Government​
Mestnichestvo government reform now appears under the Age of Discovery column, matching the age of the advance that unlocks it.
Fixed the "Settle the Frontier" cabinet action not upgrading the RGOs of the settled locations on completion, unlike normal colonization.
Fixed subjects created by a country with the "Reformed Nahua Society" reform keeping the "Altepetl" reform and its doom mechanic instead of adopting it.
Fixed the "Barter Exchange" policy showing its requirements without any text in the "Coinage Law" tooltip.
Fixed the "Reformed Tawantinsuyu Government" reform being available to Quechuan countries that never formed Inca, and being removed along with the "Tawantinsuyu Monarchy" reform when Crown Power dropped below 60%.
The Ottoman "Coffee House Espionage" government reform is no longer a Major Reform.
Fixed the Consolidated Corruption privileges showing "CURRENT_VALUE" instead of the current Legitimacy in their tooltips.
The Pearl of the Mediterranean reform now grants one extra Naval Governor instead of two.

Economy and Production​
Fixed the "Market Charter" urban right only raising the Marketplace level cap and not that of its upgrades ("Merchant's Quarters", "Grand Marketplace" and "Commerce Center").
Replaced the flat Institution Growth of the "Bou Inania Madrasa" in Fez with a +25% Institution Growth modifier, so it no longer spreads institutions into Fez on its own.

Society​
Fixed the Orthodox "Loan Icon" action allowing the same Icon to be offered to several countries at once, before the first one answered.

Diplomacy​
Fixed the Red Turban Rebellions "Demand Annexation" action making nations more likely to accept when Yuán has a high opinion of them; acceptance now depends on the recipient's opinion of the actor.
Fixed large Samanta subjects being impossible to promote to Maha-Samanta because of their tax base.

Military​
Increased the transport capacity of the Maghrebi "Barbary Fusta" levy ships from 60 to 150 men each, as their low capacity made it very hard to ferry armies across the Strait of Gibraltar.
Fixed the "Tuareg Veiled Cavalry" and "Muslim Riders" units not showing up for recruitment until a location meeting their culture or religion requirement was selected.

Geopolitics​
The "Flota de Nueva España" and "Galeones de Tierra Firme" treasure fleets now load silver and gold produced by your subjects in the region, not just by the owner of the loading port.
Fixed the "Flota de Nueva España" and "Galeones de Tierra Firme" expeditions showing a departure gold requirement lower than the amount actually charged on launch.
Added a 5-year cooldown to the "Flota de Nueva España" and "Galeones de Tierra Firme" expeditions, tracked separately for each fleet.

Advances​
"Agile Exportation" now increases the export efficiency of building-based countries, instead of a market-owner modifier they could never use.
Moved the "Bǎochuán", the unique heavy ship of the Chinese culture group, from the Age of Discovery to the Age of the Renaissance, so it no longer requires the "New World" advance and uses Age of the Renaissance stats.

Achievements​
The Getting Things Strait achievement now also counts Naval Batteries, so it can still be earned after Coastal Forts are upgraded or replaced.

AI​

Government​
AI countries now fill empty government reform slots within a few months instead of waiting up to 8 years.

Diplomacy​
The AI no longer accepts Humiliate as an easy way out of a war it is winning, and no longer treats a peace offer without its war goal as containing everything it wants.
The AI now demands the province it declared war for in its peace offers, even when that province is outside its usual conquest targets.

Economy​
AI countries now always consider trading with neighboring markets, so countries in markets such as Moskva, Kyiv and Kazan no longer leave trade capacity unused.

Interface​

Map Modes​
Convert Province now uses a dedicated map mode with a legend, coloring provinces from red (0%) to green (100%) by the selected religion's share.
Migration map mode arrows now show immigration as well as emigration, for the selected location or, with none selected, for your own country.

Alerts​
The Open Bureaucracy Slots alert now disappears when you cannot afford any bureaucracy.

Panels​
Force Embargo target list now shows a sortable Will Accept column, and no longer lists already-embargoed countries as a disabled row with a blank "NOT giving to" requirement.
Exiled units now show the exile flag icon in the unit overview header instead of a missing-icon placeholder.
The Union panel's Main Actions now refresh while the panel is open, not only on save load.
The search bar and location filters now work when recruiting army regulars or ships.
The foreign country panel now shows the succession law of countries whose law is locked, such as Papal Conclave and Admiral Election.

Tooltips​
Long country names and large values are no longer cut off in the Production Value by Country market tooltip.
Trade capacity tooltip now compares your trade income per capacity against building upkeep per capacity.
War score breakdown lines for bank and trading company buildings now name the company whose buildings are held, instead of reading like an occupation of your own land.
Camel levy tooltips now state that only pops in locations with Camels as their raw good can be levied, which explains why fewer Mehari and Lamellar Camelry can be raised than the listed percentage suggests.
Celestial Laws tooltips now show the leader of the Middle Kingdom only the policy effects that apply to them.
The monthly conversion tooltip now explains that its base value is a per-pop rate scaled by social class, and that the panel total is next month's estimate summed over all pops of other religions.
Added a line to the tooltip of international organizations such as the "Ilkhanate" explaining that, while at war, all members and the enemies of any member have military access in every member's territory.
The tooltip explaining why pops support an estate rebellion now makes clear that pops rebel based on their own satisfaction rather than their estate's, and lists the locations with the most rebelling pops along with the satisfaction they need to stop rebelling.
Fixed empty location names in the Reformation movement "Locations affected" tooltip.
Expedition tooltips now list leader requirements, such as the admiral requirement of the treasure fleets.
The Call to Arms tooltip no longer lists each "cannot intervene" reason twice or names the wrong side in the allied-in-another-war reason.
Food Decay breakdown now shows Food Preservation Efficiency as a separate reduction after the decay modifiers.
Bureaucracy Maintenance is now shown in green in the scaling breakdown of negative bureaucracy modifiers.
Press Private Regiments now lists the regiments it raises in its effects.

Other​
The Exemption from Tariffs peace demand now shows proper text in the country tooltip, the diplomatic message popup and the news log.
Imminent battle prediction now includes exiled armies whose exile lifts at the battle location.

3D Art​

Buildings​
The Across the Pillars 3D buildings no longer appear with the DLC disabled.

Miscellaneous​

Agenda​
Fixed the Knights Hospitaller Agenda window's historical context starting with "The the Knights Hospitaller".
