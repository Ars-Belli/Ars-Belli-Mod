# Mercenary-only unit types. Stats are written out in full so they do not copy the shared age template.
# Copying that template also overwrites it, which was capping and age-locking the regular unit.
# These are special regiments with limit 0. The army builder leaves that combination out of recruitment.
# No age key. A current-age mercenary of the same category retires the previous professional. location_potential still limits hiring to that age.
# The zero naval stat under hide is only a badge marker for the army interface. It is not an age.
