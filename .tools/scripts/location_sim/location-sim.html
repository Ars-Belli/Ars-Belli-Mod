    Food surplus 100% means the province makes a quarter more food than it eats (+0.15% growth a year); food storage 100% means two years of food stored (+0.1%). A starving province gets neither and loses 5% a year. Burgher trade is added to wealth and goes to the burghers. Levies recovered below 100% means levies were raised recently.

    The projection as a table, every 12 months

    What is a building level worth?

    The change from one more level of each building this location owns, everything else as set. The new level is staffed like the others: the same staffing %, or from the pops when staffing is blank, so it can lower another number by taking workers from another building. Upkeep is what the owner pays.

    Manpower. Only buildings give a location manpower: armories and barracks in towns and cities, training fields in rural settlements, the sergeantry in the capital, each scaled by how well soldiers staff it. That is multiplied by (1 + local Monthly Manpower), by control (counted as at least 10%) and by (1 + the country's Manpower). Max manpower holds five years of it. Sailors work the same way with docks.

    Levies. Each pop raises the strongest levy unit its country has: 2% of peasants, laborers and soldiers (and burghers from the Age of Discovery) as infantry, 10% of nobles as cavalry. Pops of a culture that is not primary or accepted raise a quarter. The Levy Size modifiers add up into one multiplier, control counts as at least 10%, and an estate below 25% satisfaction raises nothing.

    Wealth and tax. Wealth is what the RGO sells plus the profit of producing buildings (losses count). The Tax Base is wealth × control. Estates share it by their pops (nobles weigh 100 each, burghers 40, clergy 25, commoners 1); the commoners keep their Peasant Enfranchisement part and the nobility takes the rest. The crown takes each estate's tax rate × Tax Efficiency.

    Growth. Population Growth from the location and the country, plus up to +0.15% a year for a food surplus in the province and +0.1% for full food stores. From 60% of capacity growth slows. Births go to primary, accepted and tolerated pops, in proportion to their size.

    Control. Max control comes from proximity (× 0.75), the capital, rank, integration and pop satisfaction. Below the max, control rises by Monthly Control each month; above it, it falls by up to half a point a month.

    Food. A location makes food from its RGO (if the raw material is food), from peasants and laborers without a job (0.8 each) and from food buildings, scaled by Monthly Food and by the rank (towns −10%, cities −20%, megalopolises −30%). Every thousand pops eat a month: nobles 20, clergy and soldiers 5, burghers 4, peasants, laborers and slaves 1.

    Approximate or left out. The RGO size when no save gives it, tax efficiency (Spain's save pays about 10% more than its modifiers explain), burgher trade, the province's food store and its trade with the market (they need the whole country), and prices (the scenario's market or the base prices; they stay put in the projection). Input shortages of buildings come only from a save.

    Source. “EU5 Location Mechanics (1.4.4).md” in the Ars Belli repository, from eu5.exe 1.4.4 and the game files. Checked against Spain's 522 locations in a save of 1 April 1547: wealth within 0.5%, births within 0.7%, Monthly Manpower 6% low.
