    A day of battle. The first 3 days are the bombard phase: only artillery in the battle line fires, up to the battle frontage of guns per section, each with a 20% × (1 + bombard efficiency) chance a day. Dice re-roll when it ends. After that, each day reserves move up (chance combat speed × 0.1, a frontage budget of the battle width), regiments not yet engaged engage (60% + initiative bonus up to 30% + 3% per day of battle), engaged regiments hit the enemy section facing them, everyone loses 0.03 morale, and losses land at once. Dice re-roll every 4 days.

    Damage per hit. (raw die + 5 + dice modifiers, capped at 15) × 0.12, then × 0.04 for strength and × 0.25 for morale; morale × attacker men/max and × attacker size ÷ target size (0.25–4); × (1 + unit power); × flanking ability on flank attacks; × (1 + discipline) ÷ (1 + target discipline); × religion modifier; × combat power; ÷ (1 + tactics gap) when the target has more; × levy factor for a levy hitting a regular; strength × attacker men; × (1 − 0.5 × target experience/100); × target damage taken (cavalry 0.30); × combined arms; secured flanks; × terrain (unit's combat values for the location's topography, vegetation, climate, coast and river); strength/morale damage done and taken; +10% strength and +20% morale damage against a target not yet engaged.

    Ending. At the start, a side with 10 times the enemy's men (levies at their levy factor) wipes it out at once, whatever the morale. During the first 4 days, a side whose battle line has broken (morale at or under 0.05) is wiped out if the enemy has twice its men. From day 5, a side whose battle line is empty loses and retreats, shattered if its average morale is under 0.5; only a 10:1 ratio still wipes it. So the 2:1 wipe mostly hits armies that enter a battle already broken.

    Terrain and movement. Battle frontage 10, minus terrain (mountains 4, hills and wetlands 3, forests 2–4), at least 2; three sections hold 1.25 × that each. Terrain gives the attacker minus dice (mountains 2; hills, plateau, wetlands, narrows, woods, forest, jungle 1), river crossing −1, strait −2, sea landing −1. Marching costs 0.69% of max morale a day (land morale movement cost) and armies cannot recover morale while moving.

    Combined arms. Counted in regiments across the side, auxiliaries excluded. If one category is half the regiments or more, there is no bonus at all; otherwise each category with at least 10% adds 2.5%.

    Costs. From the game's price lists (in_game/common/prices): each category has a build, reinforce and maintenance price in gold per 100 men and manpower per man (build and reinforce 1 manpower a man; upkeep 2% of the men a month for infantry, 4% for cavalry and artillery). Each is scaled by its cost modifier (e.g. cavalry build cost −20% also cuts the manpower). Upkeep is divided by (1 + maintenance efficiency), reinforcement gold by (1 + reinforce efficiency). Levies cost no manpower and 1% of the gold upkeep. Checked on a 1342 save: manpower upkeep matched exactly; the budget's gold upkeep was about 68% of the price list (a factor not yet traced).

    Deployment. Each regiment has a box from its army's formation: "balanced" deals them out C L R R L C…, "cavalry on flanks" alternates cavalry left and right and puts the rest in the centre. At the start a regiment enters its box while that section holds less than 1.25 × the frontage, else it waits in reserve. Armies arriving later go to reserve, except with the "correct section chance" (10% per military advance that gives it, and +0.1% per point of the general's administrative skill).

    Generals. Dice only come from traits (Offensive Planner +1 and others). Skills give the general's army, per point: military +0.1% discipline and +0.5% combat speed; diplomatic +0.2% morale and +1% initiative; administrative +0.1% correct section chance. The side uses the best-rated general's dice.

    Location. Frontage is 10 + terrain + location rank (town −1, city −2, megalopolis −3), at least 2, and then each side multiplies it by (1 + its possible frontage modifier: the general's administrative skill gives +0.1% a point, Absolutism +10%), so the two sides can have different widths. A fort does nothing in a field battle: no dice, no frontage. Units' terrain values (e.g. cavalry −10% in mountains) apply per terrain layer, plus coast or inland and river. No country-wide terrain modifiers exist in 1.4.4.

    Approximated. The order regiments overflow into reserve when a section is full, reinforcements' boxes, the 120-day cap, and the war of attrition (expected losses per battle, one manpower pool per side). Not modelled: naval combat.
