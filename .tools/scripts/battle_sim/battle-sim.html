    A day of battle. The first 3 days are the bombard phase: only artillery fires (chance 20% × (1 + bombard efficiency) a day). After that, each day reserves move up (chance combat speed × 0.1, a frontage budget of the battle width), regiments not yet engaged engage (60% + initiative bonus up to 30% + 3% per day of battle), engaged regiments hit the enemy section facing them, everyone loses 0.03 morale, and losses land at once. Dice re-roll every 4 days.

    Damage per hit. (raw die + 5 + dice modifiers, capped at 15) × 0.12, then × 0.04 for strength and × 0.25 for morale; morale × attacker men/max and × attacker size ÷ target size (0.25–4); × (1 + unit power); × flanking ability on flank attacks; × (1 + discipline) ÷ (1 + target discipline); × religion modifier; × combat power; ÷ (1 + tactics gap) when the target has more; × levy factor for a levy hitting a regular; strength × attacker men; × (1 − 0.5 × target experience/100); × target damage taken (cavalry 0.30); × combined arms; secured flanks; × terrain (unit's combat values for the location's topography, vegetation, climate, coast and river); strength/morale damage done and taken; +10% strength and +20% morale damage against a target not yet engaged.

    Ending. At the start, a side with 10 times the enemy's men (levies at their levy factor) wipes it out at once. From day 5, when a side has no regiment left with morale of at least 0.05, it loses; if the winner then has twice its men, the loser is wiped out, otherwise it retreats, shattered if its average morale is under 0.5.

    Terrain and movement. Battle frontage 10, minus terrain (mountains 4, hills and wetlands 3, forests 2–4), at least 2; three sections hold 1.25 × that each. Terrain gives the attacker minus dice (mountains 2; hills, plateau, wetlands, narrows, woods, forest, jungle 1), river crossing −1, strait −2, sea landing −1. Marching costs 0.69% of max morale a day (land morale movement cost) and armies cannot recover morale while moving.

    Approximated. Deployment order inside a formation, the combined-arms bonus (2.5% per category holding 10–50% of the side), reinforcements joining as reserves, the 120-day cap. Not modelled: generals' traits other than dice, country-specific per-terrain unit modifiers, battles in a fortified location, naval combat.

    Source. “EU5 Combat Mechanics (1.4.4).md” in the Ars Belli repository; unit stats from the 1.4.4 unit types and Ars Belli’s overrides.
