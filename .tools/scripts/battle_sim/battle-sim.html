Ars Belli Battle Simulator

Pit two armies against each other with the land combat rules of Europa Universalis V 1.4.4, read from the game code. Run hundreds of battles to see the odds, watch one battle day by day, take a single hit apart, and measure what a stat is really worth.

Dice re-roll every 4 days, regiments engage by initiative, reserves move up by combat speed, and every hit runs the game's damage formula. A few steps the code reading did not cover are approximated; they are listed at the bottom.

Frontage is 10 on open ground; mountains −4, hills and wetlands −3, forests −2 to −4, never below 2. Each of the three sections (left, centre, right) holds 1.25 × frontage of fighting regiments.

    Show the results as a table

    Damage per hit. (raw die + 5 + dice bonuses, capped at 15) × 0.12, then × 0.04 for strength and × 0.25 for morale. Then: morale × attacker's current/max strength and × attacker size ÷ target size (0.25 to 4); × (1 + unit power); × flanking ability on flank attacks; × (1 + attacker discipline) ÷ (1 + target discipline); × combat power; ÷ (1 + tactics gap) when the target has more tactics; × levy factor when a levy hits a regular; strength × attacker's men; × (1 − 0.5 × target experience/100); × target category damage taken (cavalry 0.30); × combined arms; secured flanks; category damage done/taken; +10% strength and +20% morale damage against a target that is not engaged.

    Each day. Reserves move into the section with the most room and enemy pressure, with a chance of combat speed × 0.1, until a daily frontage budget runs out. Regiments not yet engaged engage with a chance of 60% + initiative bonus (up to 30%) + 3% per day. Engaged regiments hit the section facing them (left faces right) and keep their target while it stands. Everyone loses 0.03 morale a day. Regiments below 5% morale leave the field.

    Levies. −20% discipline, and when they hit regulars their damage × (0.75 + 0.25 × levy combat efficiency), capped at 1.0.

    Approximated. Artillery bombardment is modelled as a 20% daily extra hit during the first 3 days. The order of steps inside a day, deployment by formation, the combined-arms bonus (2.5% per category holding 10–50% of the army), the end of battle (a side loses when nothing of it can fight; overrun when the winner has twice its strength) and the 90-day cap are close readings, not exact ones. Terrain, river and religion combat modifiers, generals' traits other than dice, and retreat to a neighbouring province are left out.

    Source. Formulas and addresses are in “EU5 Combat Mechanics (1.4.4).md” in the Ars Belli repository. Values: game 1.4.4 defines, unit categories and age unit templates.
