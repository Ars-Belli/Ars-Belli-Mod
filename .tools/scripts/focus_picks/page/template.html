Ars Belli Focus Picks

From the Age of Renaissance on, every country makes two picks each age: one Economic and one Military focus, each chosen from three branches. A pick unlocks that branch's advances in the tech tree. The branches you don't pick stay hidden.

  • Where to pickThe Focus Picks button in the top bar, next to the Tier List.
  • Changing your mindUp to 3 changes per game, and only before you research an advance from the pick you're leaving.
  • Age of TraditionsNo picks. The first pair comes with the Renaissance.
  • AI countriesNever pick a focus.

Work in progress: these are the values on the focus-picks development branch, not yet in a released version of the mod.

Sabotage Fort

Pick a country you are at war with, then one of its locations with a fort. Every soldier pop there becomes peasants, and the fort stands unmanned until the location raises new soldiers.

Unlocked by
Espionage focus, Sabotage Fort advance
Cost
50 spy network, plus gold equal to 3 months of the target's income (at least 25)
Cooldown
2 years for the user. A sabotaged country can't be targeted again by anyone for 2 years.
Limits
Wartime only. Capitals can't be targeted. The victim is told who did it and where.

Infiltration

Both versions lift the fog of war over the target's whole territory and cost the same. The focus version lasts eight times as long.

Infiltrate Administration
Espionage focus. 50 spy network, 24 months.
Lesser Infiltration
Renaissance tree, open to everyone. 50 spy network, 3 months.

Steal Technology

Take 20% of the target's current research progress. The target loses nothing.

Unlocked by
Espionage focus, Industrial Espionage advance
Cost
100 spy network
Targets
Countries with more advances researched than you

Change log

Newest first. Times are Central European time (CET, or CEST during summer time).

    Combat stats, including army and navy upkeep, are marked in each list. Hover over a bonus, or tap it on a phone, to see what it does; lines marked ? explain what the advance unlocks.
