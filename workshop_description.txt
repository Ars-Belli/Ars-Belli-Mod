[h1]Ars Belli Multiplayer  Mod[/h1]

[i]A gameplay-focused mod for Europa Universalis V, built primarily for multiplayer sessions.[/i]

Ars Belli adds a custom power-ranking system, diplomatic alliance limits, and an extensive military/diplomacy rebalance. Supports game version 1.2.*.
Server link: https://discord.gg/ZxbBhu9W

[hr][/hr]

[h2]Pops & Setup[/h2]
[list]
[*]Rebalanced the starting setup for the Hundred Years War (more French vassals, Independent Burgundy and flanders, better Scotland, adjusted borders and castles), Spain (Castille nerf), Bohemia, Eastern Europe (Kyiv and Moscow boost), Mamluks.
[*]Remade India and China setup for multiplayer balance.
[*]Pop adjustments across Asia: Timurid, Delhi, Khmer, Ayutthaya, Ming (Middle Kingdom), and the Lordship of Pale.
[/list]

[h2]Multiplayer Power Ranking[/h2]
[list]
[*]New [b]"Ars Belli: Multiplayer Mechanics"[/b] game rule (top of the rules list, default [b]ON[/b]). Flip OFF to keep all other Ars Belli features but disable the MP ranking system, AP/DP/GR limits, and every UI element tied to them — useful for single-player.
[*]Every country is classified into a tier: [b]Great Power, Major Power, Normal, Small, Minor[/b].
[*]A country's "Power Score" is calculated from population, economy, trade income, manpower & military strength, navy, and controlled/vassal population.
[*]The score is smoothed over a 12-month target so rankings don't swing wildly month to month; tooltips show the projected score.
[*]Click the country rank label in the top bar to open a tier-list panel listing every country in each rank with flag, name, and power score. The panel toggle is per-player in MP (no longer opens for everyone when one player clicks).
[*]The foreign country diplomacy panel shows the viewed country's MP rank, power score, and diplomatic point usage/limits.
[*]Game rules adjust the number of GPs (up to 12), Majors (up to 12), Alliance Point limit, Defensive Point limits per tier, and alliance costs per tier.
[/list]

[h2]Diplomatic Limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]Alliance Points (AP) and Defensive Points (DP) cap how tangled the diplomatic web can get; exceeding the AP limit can apply a severe penalty (-90% tax income, -90% trade income, -90% research speed, -40% land morale, no war declarations) — penalty is now [b]OFF by default[/b] via game rule.
[*]Default base AP limit raised from 6 to 7.
[*]A country that is in both an alliance and a defensive league with you counts only once — defensive points take priority.
[*]Personal unions count as defensive, [b]unless[/b] the union has an offensive policy active (Possible / Automatic / Assured Offense), in which case both members consume Alliance Points instead — defensive unions still cost DP.
[*]Colonial nations and their overlord now consume Alliance Points on both sides (when both are players).
[*]Only Great Powers and Majors get Guarantee Relations; everyone else gets 0. Each guarantee counts as 1 relation regardless of rank.
[*]Hegemons can now ally each other.
[*]Toggleable alert when over the alliance limit (default OFF), enabled from the tier list UI.
[/list]

[h2]Diplomacy Changes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Break Others' Guarantee:[/b] forcibly remove a guarantee placed on you. Requires peace and creates a truce on use.
[*][b]Worsen Opinion:[/b] new action that worsens YOUR opinion of another country by -100, decaying over 10 years.
[*][b]New "Pay for" actions:[/b] military access, fleet basing, trade access, food access, fondaco rights with a gold slider to sweeten the offer. AI acceptance scales with the gold offered. Military Access and Fleet Basing Rights auto-accept unless the AI is a rival. Country picker is filtered to diplomatic range.
[*][b]Unconditional Surrender[/b] action from Gekkarino's Grand EU5 Rebalance and Complete Overhaul Mod.
[*][b]Vision Sharing:[/b] no longer requires non-negative opinion on either side — share vision with countries you dislike.
[*][b]Institution-spread exploits closed:[/b] Knowledge Sharing is disabled outright, and Alliance + Fleet Basing Rights no longer spread institutions between the parties (they were a spammable instant-action vector in MP).
[/list]

[h2]Personal Unions[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Force Break Union:[/b] junior partners — and any member of a federal union — get a button in the union panel that generates a random new ruler and instantly breaks the union. Costs 20 legitimacy and has a 50-year cooldown.
[/list]

[h2]Characters & Cabinet[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Dismiss Head of Cabinet:[/b] new character interaction. Returns your current Head to a regular cabinet seat for 5 government power.
[/list]

[h2]War & Military Rebalancing[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Ticking warscore[/b] capped at 36 (up from 25 in vanilla).
[*][b]Coalition CB:[/b] attacker conquer/subjugate cost reduced to 0.75, defender raised to 1.25 — coalition wars now make the attacker's gains cheaper and the defender's harder.
[*][b]Take Down Hegemon CB:[/b] attacker and defender conquer/subjugate cost set to 0.60 (vanilla 0.25/0.25) — peace deals against a hegemon are closer to normal scale.
[*][b]Religious-war wargoals neutralized:[/b] Heretic and Religious Conquer Province now use neutral 1.0 attacker/defender conquer + subjugate cost (vanilla loaded them +75% / +20%).
[*][b]Location warscore tuning:[/b] tax-base contribution reduced to 20% of vanilla; lack-of-control impact bumped to +0.2 from age 1 onward — unoccupied land worth less, occupied land more.
[/list]

[h3]Fort Rebalancing[/h3]
[list]
[*][b]Fort limit rebalance:[/b] significantly lowered fort limit to make wars less static, base limit of 5. Fort techs give a flat +3; locations give +0.05 each. Country rank, city count, and nobility power no longer grant fort limit.
[*][b]Defensiveness:[/b] gives half as much % fort limit (25%). No longer gives a combat speed bonus or an army movement speed penalty — instead grants a scaling -10% siege ability.
[*][b]Offensiveness:[/b] gives half (or less) as much defensiveness penalty (~20%). No longer grants army movement speed — instead grants a scaling 25% logistics distance, +20% assault ability, and +20% siege ability.
[*]Being over your fort limit gives -2% fort defense per percentage point over, capped at -100%.
[*]Toggleable popup alert when over the fort limit (default ON); mute from the tier list UI.
[/list]

[h2]Supply & Logistics[/h2]
[list]
[*]Supply depot capacity per advance bumped 100x (25 -> 2500); base capacity 50 -> 5000.
[*]Transport ship food storage buffed to match logistic units age-for-age.
[*]Logistic units (camp followers -> logistics corps) carry 2.5x vanilla food: displayed storage 250 / 500 / 750 / 1000 / 1250 / 1500 across ages 1-6.
[*]"Deposit Food" button on an army now deposits 50% of the army's food fraction (vanilla 25%); supply depots themselves now stash a 50% share for armies (vanilla 10%).
[*]"Perform Army Logistics" objective tooltip flags it as banned — the objective doesn't work in the current build.
[/list]

[h2]Crusades & Jihads[/h2]
[list]
[*]Valid crusade targets are now any heathen kingdom/empire whose capital lies in Europe, North Africa, or the Middle East — in addition to vanilla holders of Catholic holy sites.
[*]Crusades and Jihads now share a global cooldown of 100 years (one per religion).
[*][b]Deus Vult crusade CB[/b] is now available to every Catholic country from game start — no longer gated behind the Deus Vult advance or Expansion of Sokoto.
[/list]
