[h1]Ars Belli Mod[/h1]

The objective of the Mod is to make all aspects of gameplay more balanced and to create a more interesting starting setup for all areas of the world. Initially built to improve our experience playing MP games on the Ars Belli community server, now expanded with customizable rules to tailor the experience exactly to your needs - including full Single Player compatibility!

[b]A new campaign starts on Friday, 21 August 2026[/b] - sign up on our Discord!
[b]Join the community:[/b] https://discord.gg/e7T8Ju4Ewv
Supports game version 1.3.*

[h2]The Starting Setup[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]India and China[/b] now have dozens of playable tags, many with full Unique Advancement sets based on the countries' original EU4 National Ideas.
[*][b]Fully playable Japan[/b] with over 30 clans and a functional Shinto religion, loosely based on the Sengoku Jidai period.
[*][b]Expanded West & East Africa[/b] with several tribal Societies of Pops now playable as settled countries, with their own Advances!
[*][b]Reworked Middle East[/b] with a much less dominant Mamluks, and many formable tags in Arabia and Persia such as Hejaz, Najd, Fars, Khorasan and many more!
[*][b]Central Asia[/b] gets many playable tags, hordes with proper Advancement sets, and formables such as Bukhara, Greater Khorasan and a reworked Mongol Empire.
[*][b]Europe[/b] has seen reworks to make HRE minors more viable, France & Castile less overbearing, and a myriad of small tweaks to the starting balance to achieve a more dynamic experience.
[/list]

[h2]Diplomacy[/h2]
[i]For the detailed list of changes, check the full changelog linked above![/i]
[list]
[*]A custom [b]Power Ranking and Alliance Point system[/b] to create more balanced diplomacy in MP through in-game mechanics - toggleable on/off through campaign settings!
[*][b]Expanded diplomatic actions[/b] to give players more control over the relations between their countries. Antagonism and opinion should be player driven!
[*][b]Player-to-player interaction and choice is prioritized[/b] over RNG and forced interactions: automatic calls to arms replaced with pop-ups, a new Enforce Peace mechanic, and control over participation in Personal Unions!
[/list]

[h2]Game Balance[/h2]
[list]
[*]Changes to [b]Fort Limits[/b] to make mid- to late-game warfare more dynamic and avoid devolving into early trench warfare.
[*][b]Logistics rework[/b] to give meaningful options to supply armies in rough areas with proper investment and player interaction.
[*][b]Full Horde Rework[/b] including new units, an emphasis on raiding, and some unavoidable nerfs to Horse Archers.
[*]Various tweaks to outlier units where necessary. [b]New unit types[/b] instead of simple numerical buffs for countries and regions that need a leg up against stronger opponents!
[/list]
