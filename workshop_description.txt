[h1]Ars Belli Mod[/h1]

[b]Full changelog:[/b] https://docs.google.com/document/d/19thih-iu9gJ5U7drDDu41PD5In_rObTvTpubbNE1pCM/edit

The objective of the Mod is to make all aspects of gameplay more balanced and to create a more interesting starting setup for all areas of the world. Initially built to improve our experience playing MP games on the Ars Belli community server, now expanded with customizable rules to tailor the experience exactly to your needs - including full Single Player compatibility!

[b]A new campaign starts on DATE[/b] - sign up on our Discord!
[b]Join the community:[/b] https://discord.gg/e7T8Ju4Ewv
Supports game version 1.4.* (built on 1.4.9)

[hr][/hr]

[h2]The Starting Setup[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]India and China[/b] now have dozens of playable tags, many with full Unique Advancement sets based on the countries' original EU4 National Ideas.
[*][b]Fully playable Japan[/b] with over 30 clans and a functional Shinto religion, loosely based on the Sengoku Jidai period.
[*][b]Expanded West & East Africa[/b] with several tribal Societies of Pops now playable as settled countries, with their own Advances!
[*][b]Reworked Middle East[/b] with a much less dominant Mamluks, and many formable tags in Arabia and Persia such as Hejaz, Najd, Fars, Khorasan and many more! Jerusalem can proclaim the Kingdom of Heaven, and the Qara and Aq Qoyunlu get their own Advances and formables.
[*][b]Central Asia[/b] gets many playable tags, hordes with proper Advancement sets, and formables such as Bukhara, Greater Khorasan and a reworked Mongol Empire.
[*][b]The Black Sea & Pontic Steppe[/b] rebuilt: a Genoese Kaffa and a merchant-republic Gazaria that can break free of Genoa, a playable Cossack Zaporozhia, and Advances for Crimea, Taurica, Gothia, the Nogai, Astrakhan and Kazan - plus culture regiments, Steppe Redoubts, mining decisions and a Settle the Steppe action.
[*][b]The Caucasus and Anatolia[/b] get shared and unique Advances for Georgia, Armenia, Circassia, Shirvan, Trebizond and the Anatolian beyliks, with Greater Georgia, Greater Armenia and Greater Caucasus to proclaim.
[*][b]Italy[/b] gets Advances for Venice, Florence, Genoa, Tuscany, the Two Sicilies, Sardinia, the Papal States and Dalmatia, new Italian units and formables such as Italy and Sardinia-Piedmont.
[*][b]Europe[/b] has seen reworks to make HRE minors more viable, France & Castile less overbearing, and a myriad of small tweaks to the starting balance. Around 30 German and Low Countries tags, the Swiss, France and its regions, Wales, Ireland and Iberia get their own Advances, along with new formables such as Germany, Prussia, the Netherlands, Belgium and Lotharingia.
[*][b]Colonial Nations[/b] can now form 18 New World countries - the USA, Mexica, Canada, Brazil, Argentina, Australia, the East India Company and more - each with its own flag, colours and Unique Advancement set based on its EU4 National Ideas, alongside eleven Advances shared by every colonial nation.
[/list]

[h2]Diplomacy[/h2]
[i]For the detailed list of changes, check the full changelog linked above![/i]
[list]
[*]A custom [b]Power Ranking and Alliance Point system[/b] to create more balanced diplomacy in MP through in-game mechanics - toggleable on/off through campaign settings!
[*][b]Expanded diplomatic actions[/b] to give players more control over the relations between their countries. Antagonism and opinion should be player driven!
[*][b]Player-to-player interaction and choice is prioritized[/b] over RNG and forced interactions: automatic calls to arms replaced with pop-ups, a new Enforce Peace mechanic, and control over participation in Personal Unions!
[*][b]No gold funnels between players:[/b] land sales are capped, loans are limited to 2-10% interest, and renting out your own armies as mercenaries is removed.
[/list]

[h2]Game Balance[/h2]
[list]
[*]Changes to [b]Fort Limits[/b] to make mid- to late-game warfare more dynamic and avoid devolving into early trench warfare.
[*][b]Logistics rework[/b] to give meaningful options to supply armies in rough areas with proper investment and player interaction.
[*][b]Cavalry is a real investment:[/b] horses are the limit on cavalry, with every horse unit paying five times the horses and twice the other goods, cavalry combat power retuned by age, and Stud Farms capped at one level per location.
[*][b]Full Horde Rework[/b] including new units, an emphasis on raiding, and some unavoidable nerfs to Horse Archers.
[*]Various tweaks to outlier units where necessary. [b]New unit types[/b] instead of simple numerical buffs for countries and regions that need a leg up against stronger opponents!
[*][b]Mercenaries[/b] use the units of the current age and are harder to find: shorter range and fewer companies in stable, well-controlled lands.
[*][b]Tall play rewarded:[/b] Governor Offices turn unused governor posts into reform slots and cabinet seats, the Provincial Administration develops a whole area, and manpower buildings like the Sergeantry go up to 3 levels.
[*][b]Rough terrain[/b] advances cut proximity costs only around your homeland, and the [b]Societal Values[/b] are reworked so each side of a slider is a real choice.
[*][b]New map modes[/b]: [b]Bailiffs[/b] shows where a Bailiff pays off, [b]Create Town[/b] and [b]Create City[/b] show which locations to urbanise first - cheap goods before food staples, valuable goods kept rural.
[/list]
