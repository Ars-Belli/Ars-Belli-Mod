**General Rules:**
- The game is played on speed 5 and speed 4 for pvp wars. Players who are unable to keep up may be asked to leave at the GMs discretion.
- The session start and end times are set at campaign start in CET/CEST and do not change. The game is usually saved at January 1st so game time may last a few minutes longer than indicated. 
- Signing up means a commitment to play the country to the campaigns end or until the position is irrecoverable/annexation. Leaving a viable country will affect future rostering.
- A player that was knocked out of the game may then rejoin on a new country in a region that is reasonably far away at the GMs discretion.
- Deliberately ruining the player's own nation for whatever reason is a bannable offence. What constitutes bad play or nationruining is up to the GMs judgement.
- Using exploits and abusing bugs to gain an advantage is a bannable offence. Claiming not to have known about the exploit/bug in question is not a valid defence.
- Players repeatedly pushing the boundaries and attempting to skirt the rules may be punished according to the GMs decision.

**Subs & Protection:**
- If the player is not able to be present for a session it is their own responsibility to find a substitute player for their country. In case a sub is not provided the GMs can decide to put in any other available player on the vacant country. Should the GMs consider a player unreliable (missing without warning or not providing a sub on more than one occasion) they may decide to grant ownership of the country to another player.
- In order to preserve game balance subs are required to uphold the preexisting alliances and commitments of their country unless the original player leaves different instructions. 
- A substitute player who has played a country for longer than the original player may claim ownership of the country. Such a change in ownership may also be granted by the GMs if they deem it appropriate even if this requirement has not been met.
- Once per campaign when a player is late to a session and no sub could be found their country can be granted Protection at their own explicit request if the GMs agree to it. A country granted such Protection may not be attacked by enemies, called to arms by allies in offensive wars (defensive call to arms are allowed) and cannot be offered a new alliance by third parties. Protection is not granted automatically nor is it available for the 1st session.
- It is important to clarify that Protection is not an 'ability' that can be used at the Players discretion as a tool to protect their country. It is explicitly only in the ruleset to cover cases where the Player is committed to playing their country but they will miss a session and a substitute could not be found. In cases where the GMs deem that the intention behind asking for Protection is not legitimate they can refuse to grant it at their discretion.

**Diplomacy:**
- Players cannot be vassalised under any circumstances. Tributaries and Colonial Nations (ones that were *created* as CNs during the course of the campaign) are not considered vassals in this sense.
- Players are allowed to join on Colonial Nations with the permission of the overlord country but, importantly this then counts as a full alliance relation for the overlord!
- Countries in a Federal (equal) PU are not considered a player to player vassal relation as these count as a defensive relation. 
- Passing laws that allow PUs to be called to arms in offensive wars is not allowed.
- The player who holds the Market Capital is forbidden from embargoing people whose Country Capital is within said market.
- If a player is banned from building Foreign Buildings in anothers' territory they are required to stop any ongoing constructions and are forbidden from starting new ones. It is not required to destroy buildings already constructed prior to the constructions being disallowed. In the event that there is a disagreement about this both sides are encouraged to provide screenshots.
- Buying any land from the subjects of another country is banned.
- During war using Agitate for Liberty as a third party to make vassals disloyal is not allowed. 
- It is not permitted for multiple people to Agitate for Liberty at the same time against a single vassal regardless of war or peace.

**War:**
- Breaking Truces between player countries is not allowed. Breaking a truce with AI countries is allowed even if it would bring their player allies into the conflict with whom the aggressor has a truce. The exception to this is if the AI country in question is a player's vassal or the AI ally of a country that has been granted Protection, in these cases these truces count as between players and they must be respected.
- Allying or joining a Defensive League during an already ongoing war is not allowed. Instead Enforce Peace should be used or a separate war declared.
- Using Enforce peace is allowed by only one player per war. The HRE emperor joining through the HRE mechanic is taking up this slot.
- Enforcing Peace is not allowed to be used to save AN ATTACKER from a losing war BY FORCING A WHITE PEACE. It is allowed to use the mechanic to join a war, or to use it as intended to help a DEFENDER
- The Holy Roman Emperor cannot call his allies to war when joining a war in defense of an other HRE member. Said member can call his allies as normal.
- Moving your Capital when in a war with a Take Capital wargoal is prohibited. Moving the Capital when in a war involving other wargoals is allowed.
- Slave raiding CB wars can last no more than 3 years, after which the attacker has to send a white peace offer (the defender can decide to reject this).
- When at war (by any means or circumstance) with the AI vassals of other Players **without the player overlord being involved in said war,** then these vassals must be separate peaced and removed from the conflict at the earliest opportunity. No land, treaty breaking, dismantling of forts or any other option can be asked of them, with the exception of taking gold and war reparations.

**Peace Deals:**
- When making peace between player controlled countries other demands can only be enforced once the original wargoal is taken be it for land or other peace demands.
- In the case the leader of a war is an AI country then demands against players cannot be enforced by dealing with the AI. A peace has to be made with the player directly.
- In a PVP war only annexation of occuppied land may be enforced in a peacedeal. The defeated party may accept peace offers for unoccuppied land at their own discretion.
- If a defeated country wishes to surrender unconditionally the victor has to offer a peace deal - for a surrender to count as unconditional it has to be done through the in-game mechanic, it is not enough to verbally declare the intention to surrender! Under these conditions the attacker cannot continue the war afterwards and a peace offer must be sent at once.
- Using the White Peace Imminent mechanic - or any stabhit mechanic to force a player out of a war - is only allowed to peace out the main war participant as a mechanic to terminate forever wars and NOT to peace out other participants as a pathway to victory. 
- Transferring occupations during war to peace out a target multiple times for over 100% warscore is not allowed.
- Once a side reaches 90% warscore, holds the wargoal, and the first year of the war has passed the victorious side can enforce a peacedeal up to the amount of their current warscore. This offer must be accepted by the defender as long as it abides by all other rules regarding peacedeals and is for an amount equal to or lower than the enforcers current warscore. To avoid further score changes and allow checking the peacedeal the game has to be paused.
- Do not complain afterwards if you have accepted a peacedeal that was against the rules, it will not be unmade so check what deals you agree to! The **temporary** exception with regards to 'snaking' is detailed in the below section!

**Taking Territory - 'Snaking' Rules:**
- As a general practice entire Provinces should be taken in peacedeals and not individual locations. As long as this is done, the Peace Deal is almost certainly valid!
- When directly on the border individual locations can be taken as long as these individual locations touch the **original, pre-war territory** of the conqueror.
- On the coast a **single** individual location can be taken **per area** - this is to allow naval/trading powers to take bases to operate from. If the conqueror already owns **any land** in said area, even if non-coastal, then this special exception **no longer applies.**
- Whole Provinces must be taken before proceeding with taking further land from 'deeper' in enemy territory. As an example, if the conqueror takes a full province on the border, then takes another full province after that *which does not touch the original border* but only the other conquered province, this constitutes a valid peace deal. As an other example, if warscore no longer allows the attacker to fully take the second, 'deeper' province then this is not a valid deal and other locations or peace options must be chosen. **To say it simply, only full provinces can be taken deep in enemy territory and these lands themselves must be connected to the conquerors original border by other full provinces.**
- **The Capital of the defender can only be taken in a peace deal as a full province regardless of any other rule.** If there is not enough warscore to take the full province then in this case the Capital cannot be taken, other land must be taken instead (and e.g. the capital taken in the next war).
- **All of the above rules are up to GM interpretation, if a peace is sent and is deemed invalid then an amount of territory has to be returned based on GM decision.** In general enough territory should be returned to retroactively make the result 'valid' but the entire deal can be nullified instead as well.

**Other Rules:**
- Do not build forts if an adjecent owned location already has a fort on it. The Capital is an exception to this rule. If an adjecent location owned by a vassal or a neutral country already has a fort then building a new one is allowed. If this location is then annexed by the player one of the forts still has to be demolished! If a player is in violation of this rule report it before they get involved in a war. Demolishing forts during an active war is not required in the case of the Defender, in the case of the Attacker it is up to the GMs discretion.
- In the case a player disconnects from the Lobby, making a peace deal with their country, selling or buying locations, or taking any action in general that requires their acceptance is not allowed. Taking advantage of the fact that AI will accept deals that the player themselves would not is unfair and gamebreaking and is explicitly forbidden from now on. A player country under *Protection* falls under this rule. The country of a player that is a *No Show* or that has abandoned the campaign *does not* fall under this rule.
- Transferring gold between countries as a way of providing support or as the part of other deals is an intended mechanic. The normal ways to do so are *Economic Support* and *Gifts*. Using other methods (selling Art, paying for mil acces etc.) is valid *within reason*. If the GMs deem that the intended mechanics are being fully circumvented and the activity is no longer 'within reason' they can order the offending players to stop this activity. Further circumventing this decision is grounds for being kicked.
- Player to Player deals within the various 'Treaties' discord channels are not to be upheld by GMs, they are simply a way for players to publicize their deals and thus formalize them. The only and single exception to this rule is the transfer of land for gold, or the transfer of land for other land. In these cases as long as the terms are **clearly and exactly defined, and both parties explicitly agree to the terms** then this exchange will be enforced by the GMs. This is only valid for strictly simple exchanges! Land / gold transfer that is defined as a part of other larger deals does **not** fall under this rule!
- Renaming of a country's locations to the same name is forbidden, renaming the locations of foregin countries through using exploits is likewise banned. When renaming locations only the characters of the English Alphabet can be used!
